Referrals

Now a recurring-income tracker. Each referred center earns you a percentage of their subscription that steps down over time (25% then 10% then 5%). The screen shows recurring income this month, a projection, the rate-decay timeline, and per-referral which % it's on, what it pays you monthly, and how many days until it drops. A referral detail shows the exact step dates. Then Arabic mirrored. Numbers are placeholders.
