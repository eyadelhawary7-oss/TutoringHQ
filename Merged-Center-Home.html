Center dashboard, verified

Money first, because on a verified center the balance is the thing an owner opens the app to see. Unpaid sits directly under it, since that is the only number on the screen they can act on today. The digital share is deliberate: it is the one metric that tells a center whether the switch away from cash is actually happening, and it makes the trend visible without anyone being nagged about it.
