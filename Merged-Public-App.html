Screen-Public-Auth · /signup · /login

One flow for both customers. The old build gave centers a four step wizard and teachers a single crowded card. Now both walk the same five steps and only two of them differ: what we call you, and which plans you see.

A code is sent once, ever. Then a PIN, which is what you log in with from that point on. This is the whole reason the PIN exists and the screen says so instead of just demanding six digits. Verified in the live database: users.pin_set_at, pin_attempts, pin_locked_until, and pin_reset_otps for the forgotten path.

Join by link

One link per group, for centers and teachers alike. A student opens it, sees who invited them and exactly what they are joining, and sends a request that a human approves. The form asks four things and no more, because every extra field costs completed requests: student name, student phone, parent phone and an optional note. Parent phone is not optional, because that is where every payment link goes, and a student without one is a student who cannot be billed. The line about nothing being billed until approval appears on both the form and the confirmation, and it is the same sentence the approver reads on their side. English and Arabic, each standing alone. Sample data is placeholder.

Join a group yourself

The second join path. The first one, /j/[code], waits for the center to approve. This one does not: the student proves the number is his with a one time code on WhatsApp and is in the group immediately. It still collects the parent number, because the parent receives every receipt and every alert, and a student cannot opt his own parent out of that. The code is the only one the platform ever sends for joining, and the screen says so, because a family that has been told this once is much harder to phish later. English and Arabic, each standing alone. Sample data is placeholder.

04Parent PaymentScreen-Parent-Payment.html
TutoringHQ · Reference Screen
Pay session fees · Public · Draft

Pay session fees

One page for paying any provider, a center or an independent teacher, reached by link with no account needed. The provider sets their own price and that is the figure shown; there is no breakdown of it, because the parent is buying a session, not a bundle of fees. The only thing charged separately is a small processing fee for the payment itself, stated with its formula so a parent can check it against the price in front of them, sitting where a card fee normally would. What survives from the old design is the line naming the provider as the one who sets the price and teaches, since that is the record that the money was theirs. English and Arabic.
