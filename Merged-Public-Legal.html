Privacy Policy
Version 2.0 · Updated 22 June 2026
On this page 1 · What data we collect 2 · How we use it 3 · Who controls your data 4 · How long we keep it 5 · Your rights under the PDPL 6 · Contact our DPO
1 · What data we collect
We collect what you give us: names, phone numbers, billing address, and a 6-digit PIN stored only as a secure hash, never in plain text. Center owners and staff also enter student and parent contact details on behalf of their center.
We do not process sensitive data, and we do not collect anything from third parties. Card details are handled entirely by Paymob, never stored by us.
3 · Who controls your data
If you are a student or a parent, your tutoring center is the controller of your data. TutoringHQ only processes it on the center’s instructions, as its processor. For access, correction or deletion, contact your center first.
If your center has closed or cannot help, you may contact us directly using the data rights form.
5 · Your rights under the PDPL
Under Egypt’s Law No. 151 of 2020, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing. We act on verified requests within 30 days, at no charge, and we will never ask for your PIN to verify you.
Some data may be kept where the law requires it, or to defend a legal claim, or because it sits in a backup that is purged on its next cycle.

Terms and Conditions
Version 2.0 · Updated 22 June 2026
On this page 1 · Subscription plans 2 · Payment and Paymob 3 · Grace period and suspension 4 · Refunds 5 · Acceptable use 6 · Liability
1 · Subscription plans
TutoringHQ is a subscription service, billed in Egyptian Pounds monthly or annually. Center plans run from Solo to Enterprise. Teachers have Standard, Pro and Scale, each with a 14-day free trial. Prices may change with at least 30 days’ notice.
2 · Payment and Paymob
All payments are processed by Paymob, our authorized Egyptian processor. We never collect or store your card details. Completing a payment authorizes Paymob to charge the applicable fees on our behalf.
3 · Grace period and suspension
If a payment fails, your account enters a grace period and may drop to read-only. After it expires the account is suspended, and cannot reach any data or features. Accounts left suspended too long may be blacklisted.
4 · Refunds
Subscription fees are non-refundable, except where Egyptian consumer law requires it or where we have materially failed to provide the service. If something is wrong, contact us and we will work it out.

Cookie Policy
Version 2.0 · Updated 22 June 2026
On this page 1 · What cookies are 2 · Strictly necessary 3 · Analytics 4 · What we never use 5 · How to control them
2 · Strictly necessary
These keep the platform working and cannot be switched off:
A session cookie that keeps you logged in after your phone and PIN, expiring after 24 hours.
A security token that blocks cross-site request forgery.
A cookie that remembers your language, Arabic or English.
3 · Analytics
PostHog helps us see which pages are used so we can improve. Sentry catches errors. Both are aggregated, never used for advertising. You can opt out of PostHog by emailing us.
4 · What we never use
No advertising cookies, no tracking pixels, no social-media cookies, and nothing that follows you across other websites.

For centers and teachers · 22 June 2026

1 · Who is who
Your center is the controller. TutoringHQ is the processor, acting only on your documented instructions for the duration of your subscription.
3 · Our duties as processor
We commit to:
Process only on your instructions, unless Egyptian law requires otherwise.
Keep everyone who touches the data under confidentiality.
Apply appropriate security to protect it.
5 · Breach notice
If a security incident hits your data, we notify you without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours, with what we know: what happened, who is affected, and what we are doing about it.
