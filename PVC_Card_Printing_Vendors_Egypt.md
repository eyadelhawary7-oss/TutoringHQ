## Pricing Snapshot (from listings)

- Single PVC ID/loyalty card: **~10–15 EGP** per card (e.g., dubizzle ad "PVC ID كارنيه ب 15 ج", Future PC pricing from 10 EGP).
- Bulk pricing: drops to roughly **5–7 EGP per card** at higher quantities (one listing: 10 cards / 230 EGP, scaling down to 35–45 EGP for larger batches).
- Pricing varies by: single vs. double-sided, magnetic stripe, proximity/RFID chip, lanyard inclusion, design service.
