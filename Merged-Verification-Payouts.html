Teacher payouts · Draft

Teacher payouts

The center directs its own money to a destination. It never stores anyone's bank details: each teacher holds a TutoringHQ account and maintains their own method, so a teacher who changes bank updates it themselves and the center simply picks a name. The cost difference is shown on the choice, not buried. One payout a month is free; splitting uses that free one and charges for every teacher after it, so a center decides with the number in front of them. Payment method options are placeholders until Paymob confirms what Send actually supports. English and Arabic. Sample data is placeholder.
