Teacher referrals

Same recurring model as centers: each teacher you refer earns you a share of their subscription that steps down over time (25% then 10% then 5%). The screen shows recurring income this month, a projection, the rate-decay timeline, a share link, and each referral's rate, monthly pay and days-to-drop. Payout depends on verification: verified teachers can withdraw to their bank OR spend earnings as in-app credit; unverified teachers can only spend earnings as in-app credit (toward invoices and subscription), with withdrawal locked until they verify. Both states shown, English and Arabic. Design only; draft pending legal review.
