TutoringHQ · Quick menu, everywhere Lucide icons

The same menu on every row

One sheet, one gesture. The three-dot opens it, and the actions inside change to fit the row. A student offers payment and parent contact, a session offers attendance and reschedule, a teacher offers messaging and proposals. Manager-only actions stay tagged. Shown on a student, a session, a teacher, then a student in Arabic.

03Group actionsScreen-Group-actions.html
TutoringHQ · Group actions Lucide icons

What happens when you tap a group

Two ways to reach the same actions. The quick menu slides up from the row for the top four things without leaving the list. The group page opens the full record with a pinned action bar; its More button opens the same menu. Manager-only actions are tagged. Shown: list with the menu open, the group page, then the page mirrored in Arabic.

The two, merged
