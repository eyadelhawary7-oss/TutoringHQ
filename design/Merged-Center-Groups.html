Center groups, verified

Where the billing basis and the session price that attendance bills are set. Every group collects digitally, always. There is no cash-only group, because cash is not a property of a group any more than it is a property of a student: it is a switch made for one student in one session, while taking attendance. Per session is the default basis and covers almost all real usage; monthly and bundle sit beside it for the centers that work that way, and a bundle draws down by attendance rather than by date. The sheet shows what the parent will see, so the price is never set blind. English and Arabic. Sample data is placeholder.
