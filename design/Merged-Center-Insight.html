Earn credit, not cash

A center or teacher who refers another earns a share of what that account pays, every month it pays. The reward lands as platform credit after the settlement period and is applied to their own invoices automatically. It is never withdrawn as cash, which is what stops anyone farming fake signups.

The dashboard

The hero states both sides of the deal in one line. The link carries the account’s own name so it can be shared as-is. Two numbers only, referrals and balance, because a third would dilute both.

AR · كل إحالة وقيمة خطتها

Rules

  • The reward is credit, not cash. It is applied to the referrer’s own platform invoices automatically. That is what stops fake signups being profitable, and it avoids gateway fees and VAT on money that never moves.
  • Paying is the trigger, not signing up. A referral that signs up and never pays earns nothing, and the screen says so before anyone asks.
  • State the lock before it bites. The sheet says the balance cannot be withdrawn. Discovering that at the moment of trying is how a reward becomes a complaint.
  • Expose the funnel. A referrer who can see their referral is stuck on trial will nudge them. One who cannot will contact support.
  • Do not show a third metric on the dashboard. Referrals and balance are the two that matter; anything else competes with the link.
  • Do not pay before settlement. Credit lands after the settlement window, not at the moment of payment, because a refund inside that window would leave credit already spent.
