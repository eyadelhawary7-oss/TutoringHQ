Teacher group invite and pending

A teacher's join requests belong to a group, not to the teacher, because the link itself is per group. Create a group, copy its link, share it, and anyone who uses it lands in that group's own queue. That is the opposite of the center, where one link feeds a single approval list, and it is the right shape here: a teacher already thinks in groups and the requests arrive pre-sorted. Approval is always manual, so a shared link can never quietly enrol someone. English and Arabic. Sample data is placeholder.

Class session · verified

The same class flow for a verified teacher, where both models live together. Each present student is either Digital (TutoringHQ invoices the parent, collects it, holds it in Pending, then processes it to your account on Thursday) or cash you take in person, which stays yours and you confirm manually. Pre-class is identical to the self-collect flow. No percentage or fee is shown, only take-home and status. Pressing Cash needs a quick confirm (the green button keeps Digital), and the in-progress screen shows where Digital lands. English and Arabic. Design only; verified payout is draft, pending legal review.
