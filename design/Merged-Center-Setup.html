The last Center settings page: who can log in to the center and what they can do. The list shows each staff member with their role, a permission summary, and active or paused state, plus seats used against the plan limit (extra seats are a paid monthly add-on, billed per seat, price still to be set) and a shortcut to teacher join requests (which live in My Teachers). Invite sets a role, flips six permission switches, and picks which groups they manage. English then Arabic. Staff names and numbers are placeholders. Design only.

My Teachers

Where a center runs groups with outside teachers on a revenue split. Four tabs: Teachers, Requests, Slots, Add. Teachers shows each teacher's groups with the center's cut and the monthly split; Requests carries the proposal, the counter-offer, and the offer history; Slots are open teaching times a teacher can claim; Add invites one in. The last frame mirrors the Teachers tab in Arabic. This is design-only: the split is shown, never calculated here.
