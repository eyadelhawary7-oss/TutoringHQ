Center attendance, verified

Attendance is the billing trigger. Every student is digital by default; cash is not a kind of student but a choice made for one session, so the method chip is a switch that flips back just as easily. Three separate tap targets per row: the box marks present or absent, the chip switches method, and the name opens student details. Students covered by a monthly plan or bundle are never charged twice. English and Arabic. Sample data is placeholder.
