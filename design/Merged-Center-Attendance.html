Center attendance, verified

Attendance is the billing trigger. Every student is InstaPay by default; cash is not a kind of student but a choice made for one session, so the method chip is a switch that flips back just as easily. Three separate tap targets per row: the box marks present or absent, the chip switches method, and the name opens student details. Students covered by a monthly plan or bundle are never charged twice. English and Arabic. Sample data is placeholder.

Everyone is InstaPay unless you say otherwise

Marking the room present sets every student to InstaPay in one action. Tapping a student switches that one to cash. There is no method picker and no extra screen, because the common case should cost nothing.

Default InstaPay, tap for cash

The default is the method that generates an invoice, because that is the one the platform needs to know about. Cash is the exception and it takes one tap. A center with InstaPay switched off in settings sees no pills at all and the screen is a plain checklist.

After the session, the switch only runs one way

Cash can still become InstaPay, because no fee has been charged yet and the parent simply gets an invoice. InstaPay cannot become cash, because the parent has already been invoiced for the 10 EGP processing fee and paid it. Reversing would let a center pocket a fee the parent funded. The screen says why rather than greying a button silently, or staff read the block as a bug.
