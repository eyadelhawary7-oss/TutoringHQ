Teacher WhatsApp

The teacher portal had no WhatsApp screen at all, despite teachers getting bundled credit, buying the same packs at the same rate, and messaging parents every week. This is that screen. A teacher's template list is genuinely shorter than a center's, because a center runs reception, branches and staff while a teacher runs their own groups. The balance leads, since running out is the thing that hurts, and the screen separates what we pay for from what they pay for so nobody is surprised by a charge. Packs are one-time top-ups that never expire. English and Arabic. Sample data is placeholder.
