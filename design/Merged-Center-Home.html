TutoringHQ · Balance

The balance is visible wherever money is

Referral credit sits on the dashboard and on every page where an invoice is paid. It is applied before anything is charged, so a center with enough credit pays nothing and sees why. Showing it only inside the referral page would mean a center paying an invoice it already had the credit for.

On the dashboard, first

Above the day’s numbers, because it is money the account already has. The route to sending credit sits with it rather than buried in a referral page.
