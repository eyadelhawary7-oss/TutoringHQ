04Center ReceiptsScreen-Center-Receipts-Verified.html
TutoringHQ · Reference Screen
Center receipts

Center receipts

Two lists, deliberately not merged. Payment confirmations record what a parent transferred and to whom; they are proof of payment, not tax documents. Tax documents are the only items with legal standing, and they cover the platform's own fees alone — the 10 EGP service fee and the 20 EGP processing fee — never the tuition, because the tuition never passes through us. Merging these into one "receipts" list is what would make the whole arrangement incoherent to an inspector, so the split is structural rather than cosmetic. There was a third list, payout statements, recording what reached the center from the platform; nothing reaches the center from the platform any more, so it is gone rather than empty. English and Arabic. Sample data is placeholder.

05InstaPay InvoiceScreen-InstaPay-Invoice.html
The invoice, with the processing fee on it

The processing fee line is always visible to the parent. It is the platform processing fee at its lower rate: 20 EGP on every invoice we issue, 10 EGP on a tuition invoice because the parent funds that one. It attaches at invoice creation rather than at method selection. That is deliberate: it means the fee follows the invoice, so a center cannot avoid it by switching the record to cash afterwards.

08InstaPay Batch ListScreen-InstaPay-Batch.html
TutoringHQ · Batch list

One screenshot, many payments

The second way evidence arrives. A center or teacher filters their own InstaPay history to a date range, screenshots the list, and uploads it. Whichever side arrives first creates the record; the second matches into it. Neither side is required, because most payments will only ever have one.

The list upload

A batch row carries amount, sender name and timestamp but no reference number, so uniqueness is sender plus timestamp plus amount. A row that already exists from a parent upload matches into it rather than creating a second record. Anything ambiguous waits for a human rather than guessing.

10InstaPay Fee TotalScreen-InstaPay-FeeTotal.html
TutoringHQ · Service fees

Ten pounds a receipt, twenty pounds an invoice

The 10 EGP service fee is funded by the parent on each tuition invoice and accrues through the month. It reaches TutoringHQ once, on a platform invoice, and every platform invoice carries the standard 20 EGP processing fee, VAT inclusive. That makes where the fees ride a real choice worth twenty pounds.

What accrued this month

Charged only on confirmed payments, never on a failed upload or a retry. A parent uploading four times generates one fee, and cash costs nothing at all.

How the fee reaches us, and why it matters

Every invoice the platform issues to a center or teacher carries a 20 EGP processing fee, VAT inclusive. The 10 EGP on a parent’s tuition invoice is a different thing entirely and does not carry it. So the service fees can ride on the next subscription invoice as one line, paying the processing fee once, or arrive as their own invoice and pay it twice. The screen shows both totals rather than describing the difference.

Rules

  • Credit stays credit. It cannot be withdrawn as cash on either side. That is what keeps this outside money transmission and away from a licence question.
  • Show the balance after sending, before the button. Not after the transfer, when it is too late to reconsider.
  • Show what kind of account and where. Two centers can share a name, and a transfer to the wrong one is not recoverable.
  • Do not allow sending more than the balance. No overdraft, no negative credit, no borrowing against future referrals.
  • Do not hide the lock on this screen. This is exactly where someone expects cash to be possible, so it is restated rather than assumed known.

Credit earned, credit sent, credit received, credit spent on an invoice. One ledger, filterable, with a reference on every line. Two businesses moving money between them will eventually disagree about whether a transfer happened, and a confirmation screen that has been dismissed is not a record.

Rules

  • Every movement carries a reference. Earned, sent, received, spent. Both parties see the same string.
  • Neither side can delete a line. A ledger a user can edit is not evidence of anything, and this exists precisely for the moment two businesses disagree.
  • Show direction, not just sign. An arrow and a colour read faster than a minus, particularly across two scripts.
  • Do not fold invoices out of this ledger. Credit spent on an invoice is a movement like any other; hiding it makes the balance appear to drop for no reason.
