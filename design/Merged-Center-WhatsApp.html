Custom pack · the flow

What happens when you tap Custom: pick your amount, see the rate and total, confirm and pay through Paymob, done. One shared flow serves both portals, since center and teacher rates are identical, and both message types. Only the rate changes: notifications step down by volume, promotions price flat. Credit is a one-time top-up and never expires. Numbers are editable placeholders. English, with the confirm step also mirrored in Arabic.
