Admin unpaid and failed

Every stuck payment across every provider, which is the view no centre can have of itself. The first split is the one that matters: a declined card is our problem, an unopened link is the parent's, and treating them the same wastes effort on the wrong half. Because the provider is already chasing the same parent, each row shows what they have done and when, and the bulk action excludes anyone reminded in the last two days rather than doubling up on them. Admin reminders come out of company credit, not the provider's. English and Arabic. Sample data is placeholder.
