TutoringHQ · Reference Screen
Center withdrawal · Verified · Draft

Center withdrawal, verified

One payout a month is free, so a center that takes its money once a month keeps exactly the 90% we promised. Withdrawing again in the same month is something they chose, and it is priced. Thursday is when collected money clears from Pending into Available; it is not an automatic payout, so withdrawing is always the center's own action. There is no minimum: a center can withdraw 500 if it wants to, but the fee is shown as a share of that withdrawal rather than as a bare number, so nobody does it by accident. Our own banded cost never appears, because that is our problem and splitting to avoid it is handled behind the screen. English and Arabic. Sample data is placeholder.

Center receipts · Verified · Draft

Center receipts, verified

Three lists, deliberately not merged. Payment confirmations record what a parent paid and how it split; they are proof of payment, not tax documents. Payout statements record what reached the center. Tax documents are the only items with legal standing, and they cover our commission alone, never the tuition, because the tuition was never our sale. Merging these into one "receipts" list is what would make the whole arrangement incoherent to an inspector, so the split is structural rather than cosmetic. The payout charge line is shown pending the decision on who bears it. English and Arabic. Sample data is placeholder.
