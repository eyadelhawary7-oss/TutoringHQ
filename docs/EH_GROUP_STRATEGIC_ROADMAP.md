# EH Group — 5-Year Strategic Roadmap

> Companion document to [`EH_GROUP_MASTER_CONTEXT_v24.md`](./EH_GROUP_MASTER_CONTEXT_v24.md). This roadmap captures the phased build-out of the EH Group ecosystem, anchored on TutoringHQ (CenterHQ) as the founding venture.

## Years 1–2: Foundation, Automation, and Local Dominance

- **TutoringHQ Launch:** Finalize the core infrastructure and deploy the multi-tenant SaaS across target educational hubs. Solidify the transaction-fee monetization model to establish a predictable, compounding revenue stream.
- **Hyper-Automation Integration:** Implement advanced automated workflows for client onboarding, technical support, and financial reporting. The goal is to make the entire ecosystem self-sustaining, minimizing operational friction.
- **Corporate Scaffolding:** Draft the initial legal frameworks for EH Group, preparing the baseline architecture for international IP management.

## Years 3–4: International Structuring and Global Scaling

- **The Jurisdiction Shift:** Fully execute the global corporate design. Establish the primary holding company in Mauritius to hold the global intellectual property, while routing development and operations through EHG Technology LLC in Georgia to leverage local tax efficiencies.
- **EHG Intelligence Activation:** Transition internal operational tools into proprietary commercial assets, scaling B2B intelligence services and data infrastructure.
- **Market Expansion:** Scale TutoringHQ beyond regional boundaries, adapting the codebase to support multi-currency processing and localized educational compliance frameworks.

## Year 5: Ecosystem Maturity and Capital Velocity

- **Cross-Venture Synergy:** Fully integrate the liquid capital generated by TutoringHQ and EHG Intelligence into highly optimized, automated asset-allocation models under the EH Group umbrella.
- **Institutional Handover:** Mature the day-to-day operations of the platforms to a level where they function entirely via automated systems and delegated management, freeing up maximum leverage for visionary expansion.

## Identified "Holes" & Missing Opportunities to Address

- **The Structural Hole (IP Fragmentation):** If software code for TutoringHQ is developed before the Georgia–Mauritius corporate pipeline is legally active, post-facto IP assignment can trigger unnecessary tax liabilities. **Correction:** Ensure all repository agreements explicitly state the work is done on behalf of the future corporate entity from day one.
- **The Missing Opportunity (Data Monetization):** Treating TutoringHQ purely as a management tool misses out on aggregate data value. **Correction:** Architect EHG Intelligence early on to process anonymized operational insights from TutoringHQ, generating macro-level reports that can be sold back to educational institutions as high-value market intelligence.
